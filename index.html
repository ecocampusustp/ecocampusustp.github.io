About EcoCampus

Driving the sustainable future of Cagayan de Oro City through smart, student-led waste management.

Our Mission

The EcoCampus Waste Management Initiative was established to create a 100% sustainable and zero-waste university environment. By utilizing digital mapping technology, we empower students, faculty, and the administration to dispose of waste responsibly and track our environmental impact in real-time.

Our Vision

We envision our university as the leading green institution in Northern Mindanao, serving as a model for urban sustainability where technology meets environmental consciousness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common waste management queries on campus.

What exactly goes into the Recyclable (Green) bin? +

Only clean, dry, and recyclable materials. This includes plastic bottles (PET), aluminum cans, dry paper, and cardboard.

What goes into the Residual (Red) bin? +

Residual waste is anything that cannot be recycled or composted. This includes candy wrappers, soiled food packaging, and wet wipes.
